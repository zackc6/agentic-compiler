# Instructions for Claude

Read `AGENTS.md` first (what this repo owns, the validation gate, the four-repo protocol), then `docs/SURVEY_BASIS.md` and `docs/DRIFT.md`. This is the bench: it runs Lintel and Choreo together and files **gaps** into their `gaps/` backlogs; it never designs an IR change. Before starting, read the **Open batches** table and the **Review thread** at the top of `STATUS.md`; claim a row before editing (at most three in flight). A batch (submodule bump, case addition, or measurement row) goes on `claude/<topic>-<date>`, is validated there (`./scripts/setup.sh && agentic-compiler run-all`), argued in the thread (two rounds per side), and lands only through a pull request the maintainer approves. Thread entries, Open-batches rows, and pure fact fixes may go to `main` directly. Sign commits with your agent name.
